The Origin Ritual

It All Started with a
Single SCOBY.

In 2018, our founder Leo launched a quiet revolution from a sun-drenched kitchen. Armed with nothing but an heirloom SCOBY and a relentless curiosity for ancient fermentation, he decoded the symphony of raw probiotics.

What began as a localized obsession with gut health blossomed into a movement. We don't just brew; we cultivate life. Every batch of The Fizzy is a testament to the patience of slow-crafted, organic alchemy.

Our Brewing Philosophy

01

Pure Sources

We only use spring water and organic loose-leaf teas sourced from sustainable farms.

02

Slow Cultures

Our fermentation process takes up to 21 days, allowing the flavors and probiotics to develop naturally.

03

Glass Only

Kombucha is sensitive. We only ferment and bottle in glass to preserve the purest taste and potency.

The Golden Ratio

The Master Craft

01. Temperature Sync

Precise thermal monitoring ensures each culture blooms at its optimal peak.

02. Slow Infusion

Triple-steeped botanicals deliver a depth of flavor that can't be rushed.

03. Pure Clarity

Natural sedimentation process preserves the raw spirit of the brew.

04. Living Culture

Unfiltered and active probiotics thrive in every drop of our living elixir.
